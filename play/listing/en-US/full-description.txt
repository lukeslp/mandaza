Mandaza creates endlessly varied ambient mandalas on your phone or tablet.

Tap the canvas to generate a new composition, then tune its palette, geometry, line work, motion, and glow. Mandaza creates each design on your device from a reproducible seed, so you can return to a favorite whenever you want.

Find your visual rhythm:

• Explore 12 carefully tuned color palettes
• Enter Zen mode for a quiet, full-screen presentation
• Add a customizable clock and date overlay
• Let nearby sound gently shape the motion with optional Music responsivity
• Tilt your device for subtle parallax depth
• Save finished artwork to use as a wallpaper
• Keep favorite designs and return to recent patterns
• Choose a luminous dark canvas or a softer paper look

Music responsivity uses the microphone only while the feature is active. Mandaza turns sound into a live level value on your device and immediately discards the audio samples. Nothing gets recorded, stored, or uploaded.

Mandaza has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The Google Play build makes no network requests. Your designs and settings remain on your device.
