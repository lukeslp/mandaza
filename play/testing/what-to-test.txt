Please try these flows on a phone or tablet:

1. Tap the canvas several times and confirm each new pattern animates smoothly.
2. Open Tune, change the palette, density, geometry, line width, and canvas look.
3. Enter Zen and verify the clock and date remain legible over different patterns.
4. Enable Music responsivity, allow or deny microphone access, and confirm both paths are clear and stable. No audio should be saved.
5. Turn on tilt parallax and move the device gently.
6. Add and reopen a Favorite, then use Previous history.
7. Save an image and confirm it appears in Pictures/Mandaza.
8. Rotate the device and check that the canvas and controls stay usable.

No account or sign-in is required. Please report the device model, Android
version, steps, expected result, and observed result with any issue.
