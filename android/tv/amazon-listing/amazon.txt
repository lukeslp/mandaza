== Title ==
MandazaTV

== Short description ==
Fullscreen ambient mandalas for your TV. Generate living patterns with the remote, switch palettes, and leave a quiet Zen clock on the wall. Free, no ads, no account, fully on-device.

== Long description ==
MandazaTV turns your living room into a generative ambient canvas.

Procedural mandalas draw themselves in real time across the screen—twelve curated palettes, from Aurora and Ember to Canopy and Terrain, plus luminous Void and warm ink-on-parchment Paper moods. Press Select or Play for a new composition; step left and right through palettes; press Back to hide the control dock and let the art fill the room.

A quiet Zen clock and date can sit over the pattern when you want the time as well as the light. Everything runs on the device: no account, no tracking, no ads, and nothing to buy.

Built for the Fire TV remote. Shared visual language with Mandaza on iPhone, Apple Watch, and Apple TV.

Made by Luke Steuber.

== Product feature bullets ==
- Fullscreen animated procedural mandalas for the living room

- Void and Paper canvas moods
- Remote-first: Select/Play for a new pattern, Back to hide controls
- Optional Zen clock and date overlay
- On-device only — no account, ads, or tracking
- Free
