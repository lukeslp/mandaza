Mandaza draws animated mandalas on your Fire tablet. Tap the canvas for a new design, then change its palette, geometry, line work, motion, and glow.

Choose from 12 palettes and two canvas styles: luminous lines on black or a softer paper look. Keep favorite designs, return to recent patterns, and save artwork as an image.

Zen mode pairs the mandala with a customizable clock. Adjust its appearance and position for a quiet clock or ambient display.

Optional Music responsivity lets nearby sound shape the animation. It starts off, and the app asks for microphone access only when you enable it. Sound is processed on your tablet. Audio is never recorded, stored, or uploaded.

Mandaza works offline, with no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.
