Mandaza draws animated mandalas on your Fire tablet. Tap the canvas for a new design, then change its palette, geometry, line work, motion, and glow.

Choose from 12 palettes and two canvas styles: luminous lines on black or a softer paper look. Keep favorite designs, return to recent patterns, and save artwork as an image.

Zen mode pairs the mandala with a customizable clock. Choose a digital, stacked or editorial clock, with optional seconds. Digital and editorial layouts can also show the date.

Optional Music responsivity lets nearby sound shape the animation. It starts off, and the app asks for microphone access only when you enable it. Live sound levels are measured on your tablet. Audio recordings are not saved or uploaded.

Mandaza works offline and needs no account. It contains no advertising or tracking features.
