Mandaza draws animated hexagonal-loop patterns on your phone or tablet.

Tap for a new composition, adjust its geometry and palette, save favorites, and export artwork as a PNG. Zen mode displays the art with a customizable clock and date.

Optional motion and microphone responsiveness run locally. The microphone starts only when you enable music responsiveness.

The F-Droid build has no account, advertising, analytics, direct updater, Google Play Review dependency, or Internet permission. Designs and settings stay on the device. F-Droid handles app updates.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Phone and tablet builds are included; Wear OS and TV are separate products. Full-panel flip-cover behavior is not yet verified.
